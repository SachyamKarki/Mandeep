Requirements & Business Rules

Validated with Uttam Regmi, Proprietor & Senior Surveyor, Mangaldeep Consulting.

Functional

  • Register a claim using existing insurer, client and surveyor
  • Log each site visit with date and findings
  • One invoice per claim, record payments
  • List open claims and unpaid fees
  • Flag possible duplicate claims

Non-functional

  • Staff must log in (admin / staff roles)
  • Every change kept in an audit trail
  • Runs in a browser on free software (MySQL + Next.js)

Scope

  • In: claims, visits, invoices, reports
  • Out: photos, policy data, accounting

Business rules

  • A claim has exactly one insurer, client and surveyor
  • A claim can have many visits, but one invoice
  • Claimed amount and fee must be above 0
  • Amount paid can never exceed the fee
  • Surveyor licence numbers are unique
  • Records in use by a claim cannot be deleted

Why MySQL?

  • Free and runs on an office PC
  • Enforces PK, FK, UNIQUE and CHECK rules
  • Supports views and triggers for reports and audit
  • One-command backup with mysqldump

Login and audit use 3 support tables (app_user, user_session, audit_log), kept outside the business ERD.

Key SQL decisions

  • ON DELETE RESTRICT: can't delete an insurer, client or surveyor that has claims
  • ON DELETE CASCADE: deleting a claim removes its visits and invoice
  • UNIQUE claim_id on INVOICE enforces 1 : 1
  • CHECK rules on amounts and payments
  • balance_due is calculated in a view, never stored
  • Triggers write every insert, update and delete to audit_log

Fees billed, collected and still owed per insurer

VIEW

Subquery

Claims above the average amount

NOT IN

Claims that have no site visit yet

LEFT JOIN

Surveyor workload, including surveyors with no claims

MAX + COUNT

Latest site visit and number of visits per claim

HAVING COUNT(*) > 1

Possible duplicate claims, caught by a query

Backup & Restore

A full export can be taken at any time and restored in two commands.

01 · BACKUP

Export

Tables, data, views and triggers in one SQL file.

mysqldump --databases mangaldeep_claims > backup.sql
02 · RESTORE

Import

Rebuilds the whole database on any MySQL server.

mysql -u <user> -p < backup.sql
03 · CHECK

Verified

Restored into a test database. Counts matched: 30 claims, 43 visits, 19 invoices.

SELECT COUNT(*) FROM claim;

File kept in the project folder: database/backup/mangaldeep_claims_backup.sql

What Mangaldeep gains

  • Monthly fee report in seconds, not a day of manual totals
  • Balances worked out by the database, not by hand
  • Every change logged with who made it

Limitations

  • Dummy data only; real Excel records not migrated yet
  • No claim date yet, so overdue claims can't be tracked
  • No photo or document uploads

Assumptions: 80 claims/month · average survey fee Rs 12,000 (≈ Rs 1.15 crore billed/year) · 10 min retyping per claim · 15% of fees paid late or chased · 2% duplicate entries. To be confirmed with Mangaldeep's real records.
